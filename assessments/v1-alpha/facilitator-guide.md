# Facilitator Guide — Architecture Modernization Assessment

How to run a facilitated workshop for the modernization domain.

---

## Pre-workshop (1 week before)

- [ ] Confirm participants (8–15 people, cross-functional: architects, engineers, product owners, sponsor)
- [ ] Send pre-read: link to `instrument.yaml` and 5 dimensions
- [ ] Confirm room (physical or virtual) and tools (Miro/FigJam/whiteboard)
- [ ] Send calendar invite with agenda
- [ ] Prepare score sheets (one per dimension, 5 sheets total)

## Per-dimension script (45 minutes each)

### Step 1 — Context (5 min)
- Read dimension name and weight aloud
- Explain why this dimension matters
- Reference maturity model characteristics for current band

### Step 2 — Read questions (10 min)
- Walk through each question
- Read the evidence list
- Allow clarifying questions

### Step 3 — Individual scoring (10 min)
- Each participant scores privately on a score sheet
- Facilitator collects scores on flipchart

### Step 4 — Surface disagreements (15 min)
- Highlight any questions with > 1-point spread
- Facilitator drives discussion until convergence
- Document evidence citations on flipchart

### Step 5 — Lock scores (5 min)
- Confirm final score per question
- Sum and compute percentage

---

## Cross-cut discussion (30 min)

Questions to surface contradictions:

- Where does the strategy say X but execution shows Y?
- Are patterns documented but not used?
- Are metrics collected but not acted upon?
- Are people trained but patterns ignored under pressure?

## Wrap-up (15 min)

- Summarise total score and maturity band

## Common challenges specific to modernization

- **Defensive scoring.** Modernisation programmes often score optimistically because they're politically sensitive. Counter: require evidence citations for 2+.
- **Strategy vs. execution gap.** Strategy is well-documented but execution diverges. Counter: surface the gap explicitly in cross-cut discussion.
- **Pattern attribution.** Hard to attribute outcomes to specific patterns. Counter: ask "what would have happened without this pattern?"
- **People vs. process.** Cultural change may matter more than process change. Counter: include people dimension prominently.
